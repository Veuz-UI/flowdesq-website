SCOPE

Here we draw your attention to the various privacy policies and data protection principles practiced in safeguarding your personal data for use in our products and services. When you use and interact with our websites or services, communicate with or otherwise contact us or visit our offices or attend our events, we may collect, use, share, store and process information relating to you ("Personal Data").   • Apply for employment or training programs with FlowDesQ / Veuz Concepts Please note that this privacy statement will regularly be updated to reflect any changes in the way we handle your personal data or any changes in applicable laws.

Therefore, our approach is to coordinate the contribution of several corporate disciplines - including ethics and compliance, legal, human resources, and information and physical security - to achieve our "best in class" data protection and privacy management objectives with transparency.

HOW DO WE SECURE YOUR PERSONAL DATA?

FlowDesQ runs stringent security measures throughout to ensure maximum protection of your personal data from misuse, spam or loss.

  • Encryption Standards: Data is protected through encryption mechanisms applied both in transit and at rest using industry-recognized security protocols to ensure confidentiality and integrity.
  • Access Control Framework: Role-based access control mechanisms are implemented to restrict access to personal and operational data strictly to authorized personnel who require such access for legitimate business purposes.
  • Security Monitoring and Assessments: Periodic security audits, vulnerability assessments, and compliance reviews are conducted to evaluate system resilience, identify potential risks, and ensure adherence to applicable security standards.
  • Confidentiality Obligations: All employees, contractors, and authorized third-party service providers with access to sensitive information are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Incident Response Management: Established incident response protocols enable the timely detection, reporting, investigation, and mitigation of potential security incidents affecting data or system integrity.
  • Protection Against Unauthorized Activities: Technical, administrative, and operational safeguards are maintained to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of data processed through the FlowDesQ platform.

While we take every precaution to protect your data, no system is entirely immune to risk. We encourage users to maintain strong, unique passwords and to report any suspicious activity immediately.
